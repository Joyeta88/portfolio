Governed KPI portfolio & AI enablement for Money products

Built the analytics operating model for a multi-product Money portfolio on the Executive Command Center (ECC)—a governed scorecard with 17 KPIs and 13 dimensions, backed by 30+ agent skills and plugins so Finance, Product, and Data Science pull the same numbers from the lake—not from competing spreadsheets.

Outcome: Leadership reads one Money scorecard in ECC; analysts use governed contracts and skills to explain attach rates, funded units, and revenue without re-deriving logic in chat. AI accelerates SQL and documentation—metric definitions and exec-layer lineage stay authoritative.

How AI was used end to end

  • Define — Codified ECC Money KPI expressions, numerators/denominators, and dimension playbooks into knowledge-base contracts agents can resolve before writing SQL.
  • Build — Authored and iterated product onboarding skills (pipeline catalogs, funnel steps, starter queries) and scorecard skills (metric trace, sample queries, exec-layer simplification).
  • Align — Partnered with Finance and Product on attach vs. funded vs. revenue timing so scorecard tooltips match how numbers are recognized.
  • Adopt — Office hours and demo cadence; MCP hooks to the lake and data dictionary so copilots query governed paths instead of hallucinating tables.

Context: ECC is the system of record

ECC is an in-house business management platform—not a single dashboard. Scorecards sit on a tiered metric pipeline (raw lake → exec layer → published scorecard values) with consistent dimensions across initiatives. For Money, that means refund-delivery and lending-adjacent products share one vocabulary for units, attach rates, fulfilled volume, and recognized revenue.

Standardized Money KPI portfolio

I helped stand up and document the Money vertical scorecard—the executive view for monetization during tax season. KPIs span the full portfolio:

  • Population — total units, refund vs. balance-due cohorts
  • Refund transfer — attach rate, fulfilled units, recognized revenue
  • Early refund delivery — attach, fulfilled units, revenue
  • Instant refund advance — attach, funded units
  • Partner wallet / cross-sell attaches — attach rates tied to refund cohorts
  • Pay-later lending — loans, attach rate, estimated revenue
  • Forecasts — parallel forecast KPIs for season planning

Each KPI has a documented expression, optional denominator (for rates), cumulative vs. point-in-time behavior, and tooltip text aligned with Finance recognition rules—so “attach” and “funded” are not interchangeable in narrative or in SQL.

Governance principles

  1. Stabilize tables before plugins — Curated business tables and funnel grains documented first; skills reference those sources, not ad-hoc extracts.
  2. One definition per KPI — ECC config + contracts are authoritative; AI explains and composes, it does not invent numerators.
  3. Separate experience vs. finance metrics when needed — When product funnel counts and finance recognition diverge, document parallel surfaces instead of forcing one broken number.
  4. Human review on production paths — Generated SQL goes through the same validation habits as hand-written analysis (partition filters, season alignment).
