The problem

Before governance, each team had its own extracts, SQL, and slide decks. Generative AI made that worse: fast answers, inconsistent grains, and conflicting KPIs in the same meeting. For a six-product Money portfolio during peak season, the cost was not just rework—it was loss of trust in the numbers.

Governance principles

These principles guided every decision—what we published, what we automated, and what we refused to shortcut.

  1. Governance before scale — Stabilize curated business tables and scorecard contracts before rolling out copilots team-wide.
  2. One canonical surface for leadership — Executive Scorecards hold the portfolio view; ad-hoc analysis must trace back to the same contracts.
  3. AI composes; humans certify — Agents draft SQL, memos, and diagrams. Production paths still pass human review and season validation habits.
  4. Contracts over prompts — Metric and dimension contracts resolve before query generation—reducing table hallucination and grain mismatch.
  5. Parallel surfaces when domains disagree — When product experience counts and finance reporting diverge, document both paths explicitly instead of blending incompatible logic.
  6. Adoption is a product — Skills without office hours, demos, and feedback loops become shelfware.

Executive Scorecards: what we built

Executive Scorecards are the leadership-facing layer of an in-house business management platform—not a one-off dashboard. I helped shape the Money portfolio scorecard: a single executive view spanning six to seven monetization products, with a bounded set of governed KPIs and dimensions so season reviews use the same cuts everywhere.

Work focused on structure and process, not publishing a catalog of metric names in documentation: cohort foundations, product-line groupings, planning vs. actuals views, and forecast companions—all wired through a tiered pipeline from the lake to published scorecard values.

AI adoption: steps & processes

A deliberate rollout sequence—not “turn on Copilot and hope.”

Phase 1 — Prepare the ground

  • Inventory existing tables, pipelines, and scorecard slots for the Money portfolio
  • Document grains, season boundaries, and known cross-team disagreements
  • Freeze a minimum viable contract set leadership already trusts

Phase 2 — Publish governable assets

  • Load metric and dimension contracts into the team knowledge engine
  • Align Executive Scorecard configuration with those contracts
  • Add sample query patterns for analysts (governed paths only)

Phase 3 — Ship skills & plugins

  • Product onboarding skills (pipelines, funnels, starter SQL per product line)
  • Scorecard skills (trace a leadership number back to pipeline logic)
  • SQL orchestration skill (contract resolution → guarded lake queries)
  • Domain glossary and monetization guides for shared language

Phase 4 — Connect execution

  • MCP integrations for lake query, metadata discovery, and dictionary lookup inside the IDE
  • Preflight checks so agents fail visibly when runtime prerequisites are missing

Phase 5 — Drive adoption

  • Weekly demos of new skills and trace workflows
  • Office hours with live problem-solving on real season questions
  • Feedback log → contract updates → skill patches (closed loop)
