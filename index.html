About

I'm a staff-level analytics and data engineering leader in the Boston area. At Intuit I own data foundations, analytics engineering, and AI capabilities for a portfolio of consumer fintech monetization products—standing up Executive Scorecards, metric contracts, incremental medallion pipelines, and dozens of agent skills so Finance, Product, and Data Science share one KPI language. Previously I spent five years at Amazon Pharmacy as a BI manager: co-architecting a first-party medallion platform, migrating 200+ dashboards, harmonizing 500+ KPIs, and leading 15+ analysts across 11+ partner teams.

My work follows a repeatable pattern—govern first, engineer second, enable at scale. Define what “done” means in contracts and layers; build pipelines that survive validation (incremental MERGE, lookback, dual-phase sign-off); then ship adoption (templates, CI/CD, office hours, plugins). I use AI heavily in design and review, but leadership metrics stay deterministic: the catalog and scorecard win over the chat thread.

Earlier roles at Wayfair and NetApp grounded me in experimentation, executive reporting, and analytics at thousand-user scale. The through-line is the same—trusted numbers, clear ownership, and systems that still work when the organization grows.
